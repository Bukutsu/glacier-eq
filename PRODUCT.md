# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Audio enthusiasts and maintainers tuning parametric EQ profiles for supported USB DACs on desktop and Android.

## Product Purpose

Glacier EQ provides a focused PEQ editing surface for connecting to a DAC, pulling the current filter state, refining bands and gain, managing profiles, and pushing changes back to the device.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, calm.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative audio-player chrome, marketing-page gloss, rounded consumer-app styling, and any polish that makes the dense EQ workflow slower to scan.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the dark technical workspace and cyan/blue accent vocabulary.
- Keep EQ data, profile state, and device actions immediately visible.
- Make every control feel deliberate, aligned, and keyboard-accessible.
- Favor dense clarity over decorative hierarchy.
- Use sharp corners by default. New components should use the shared `--radius-control` and `--radius-surface` tokens, which stay at `0` unless a component has a strong functional reason to differ.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion-safe transitions, and readable touch targets where the layout collapses.
